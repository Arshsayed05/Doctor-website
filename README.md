## 3. Four Core Environments

1. **Public Editorial Website**:
   * Hero section: Typography-led composition on warm ivory, headline: *"Personalised care. A simpler way to consult."*, no doctor portraits in hero, verified credential tags.
   * Service Explorer: 6 categories (Hair & Scalp, Women's Health, Digestive Health, Lifestyle, Cupping Therapy, General Ayurveda).
   * Patient Journey (3 unhurried steps).
   * Verified credentials timeline & clinical philosophy.
   * FAQs and direct clinic contact.

2. **Appointment Engine & Booking Modal**:
   * Format selection (Online Google Meet vs In-Clinic Mangalore).
   * Slot and Date picker with morning, afternoon, and evening availability.
   * Pre-consultation clinical intake form.
   * Razorpay checkout simulator.
   * Instant confirmation with generated Google Meet video link (`meet.google.com/shf-ksar-med`).

3. **Patient Dashboard**:
   * Active consultation countdown with 1-click Google Meet launch.
   * Downloadable digital prescriptions with Prakriti analysis and Ayurvedic formulations.
   * Consultation history and payment verification.

4. **Doctor & Admin Dashboards**:
   * **Doctor Station**: Today's patient queue, patient dossiers, and interactive Ayurvedic prescription builder (herbs, dosage, diet/pathya).
   * **Admin Station**: Razorpay settlement monitoring, consultation volume, and clinic capacity settings.
